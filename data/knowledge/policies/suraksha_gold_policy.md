# Suraksha Gold Family Floater Plan (Policy Wording)

FICTIONAL DOCUMENT FOR A SYNTHETIC-DATA DEMO. Suraksha General Health Insurance Ltd. does not exist.

## 1. Sum Insured and Scope

### 1.1 Sum Insured
The Sum Insured is ₹10,00,000 per policy year on a family floater basis, subject to the sub-limits in Clause 5.

### 1.2 Scope of Cover
The Company covers medically necessary In-patient Hospitalisation, Day-Care Procedures, Emergency care, Maternity (Clause 5.2) and Out-patient (OPD) consultations and investigations prescribed by a registered medical practitioner.

## 2. Definitions

### 2.1 Medically Necessary
Treatment required for the medical management of the illness or injury, not exceeding the level of care necessary for safe, adequate and appropriate care, and prescribed by a medical practitioner in line with generally accepted standards of practice.

### 2.2 Pre-Existing Disease (PED)
Pre-Existing Disease means any condition diagnosed by a physician, or for which medical advice or treatment was received, within 48 months PRIOR TO the first policy start date. A condition first diagnosed ON OR AFTER the policy start date is NOT a Pre-Existing Disease, even if the condition is chronic (such as diabetes mellitus, hypertension, asthma, atrial fibrillation or chronic kidney disease). The PED waiting period in Clause 4.2 does not apply to such a condition.

## 3. Pre-Authorisation

### 3.1 When Pre-Authorisation Is Required
Prior authorisation is mandatory for (a) every planned In-patient Hospitalisation and (b) any treatment with an estimated cost above ₹50,000.

### 3.2 Emergency Admissions
For emergency admissions, the pre-authorisation request must be submitted within 24 hours of admission; treatment shall not be denied solely because authorisation followed admission within this period.

### 3.3 Contents of a Pre-Authorisation Request
Diagnosis with code, proposed treatment, estimated cost, expected length of stay, the treating doctor's statement of medical necessity, and supporting investigation reports.

## 4. Waiting Periods

### 4.1 Initial Waiting Period
Claims within the first 30 days of the policy are not payable, except accident claims.

### 4.2 Pre-Existing Disease Waiting Period
Expenses related to a Pre-Existing Disease (Clause 2.2) are covered after 24 months of continuous coverage from the first policy start date. This waiting period applies ONLY to conditions meeting the definition in Clause 2.2.

## 5. Sub-Limits

### 5.1 Cataract
Cataract surgery is payable up to ₹60,000 per eye per policy year.

### 5.2 Maternity
Maternity expenses, including normal delivery and caesarean section, are covered up to ₹1,00,000 per delivery.

## 6. Exclusions

### 6.1 General Exclusions
Cosmetic treatment, experimental treatment and treatment for self-inflicted injury are excluded.

## 7. Claims

### 7.1 Time Limit for Reimbursement Claims
A reimbursement claim must be submitted within 30 days of discharge or completion of treatment.

### 7.2 Mandatory Documents
Claim form, discharge summary, itemised final bill, payment receipts, investigation reports and the treating doctor's prescription.
