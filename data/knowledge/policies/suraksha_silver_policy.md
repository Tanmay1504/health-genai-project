# Suraksha Silver Individual Health Plan (Policy Wording)

FICTIONAL DOCUMENT FOR A SYNTHETIC-DATA DEMO. Suraksha General Health Insurance Ltd. does not exist.

## 1. Sum Insured and Scope

### 1.1 Sum Insured
The Sum Insured is ₹3,00,000 per policy year. All admissible claims in a policy year are paid up to this amount, subject to the sub-limits in Clause 5.

### 1.2 Scope of Cover
The Company covers medically necessary In-patient Hospitalisation, Day-Care Procedures, Emergency care and Out-patient (OPD) consultations and investigations prescribed by a registered medical practitioner, as described in this wording.

## 2. Definitions

### 2.1 Medically Necessary
Treatment that is required for the medical management of the illness or injury suffered by the insured; must not exceed the level of care necessary to provide safe, adequate and appropriate medical care; and must be prescribed by a medical practitioner in line with generally accepted standards of practice.

### 2.2 Pre-Existing Disease (PED)
Pre-Existing Disease means any condition, ailment, injury or disease that was diagnosed by a physician, or for which medical advice or treatment was received, within 48 months PRIOR TO the first policy start date. A condition that is first diagnosed ON OR AFTER the policy start date is NOT a Pre-Existing Disease, irrespective of whether the condition is chronic in nature (for example diabetes mellitus, hypertension, asthma or osteoarthritis). The PED waiting period in Clause 4.2 therefore does not apply to such a condition.

### 2.3 Day-Care Procedure
A procedure that, owing to technological advancement, is completed in less than 24 hours under anaesthesia or in a day-care centre, including cataract surgery, minor surgical procedures and dialysis.

## 3. Pre-Authorisation

### 3.1 When Pre-Authorisation Is Required
Prior authorisation from the Company or its TPA is mandatory for (a) every planned In-patient Hospitalisation, and (b) any treatment where the estimated cost exceeds ₹25,000.

### 3.2 Emergency Admissions
For an emergency admission, the pre-authorisation request must be submitted within 24 hours of admission. Emergency treatment shall not be denied solely because authorisation was sought after admission within this period.

### 3.3 Contents of a Pre-Authorisation Request
The request must state the diagnosis with ICD/SNOMED code, the proposed line of treatment, the estimated cost, the expected length of stay, and the treating doctor's justification of medical necessity, along with supporting investigation reports.

## 4. Waiting Periods

### 4.1 Initial Waiting Period
Claims arising within the first 30 days of the policy are not payable, except claims arising from an accident.

### 4.2 Pre-Existing Disease Waiting Period
Expenses related to a Pre-Existing Disease (as defined in Clause 2.2) are covered only after 36 months of continuous coverage from the first policy start date. This waiting period applies ONLY to conditions meeting the definition in Clause 2.2.

## 5. Sub-Limits

### 5.1 Cataract
Cataract surgery is payable up to ₹25,000 per eye per policy year.

### 5.2 Orthopaedic Procedures
Joint replacement and other orthopaedic procedures are payable up to ₹1,50,000 per policy year.

### 5.3 Cardiac Procedures
Cardiac procedures, including angioplasty (PCI), are payable up to ₹2,00,000 per policy year.

## 6. Exclusions

### 6.1 Maternity
Expenses for childbirth (including caesarean section), pregnancy and its complications are excluded under this plan.

### 6.2 Other Exclusions
Cosmetic treatment, experimental treatment, and treatment for self-inflicted injury are excluded.

## 7. Claims

### 7.1 Time Limit for Reimbursement Claims
A reimbursement claim must be submitted within 30 days of discharge or completion of treatment. See the common Claims Procedure document for condonation of delay.

### 7.2 Mandatory Documents
Claim form, discharge summary (for hospitalisation), itemised final bill, payment receipts, investigation reports and the treating doctor's prescription.
