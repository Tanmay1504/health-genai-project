# Jan Arogya Cover — Basic Hospitalisation Package (Scheme Guidelines)

FICTIONAL DOCUMENT FOR A SYNTHETIC-DATA DEMO. The Jan Arogya Cover Trust is not a real scheme.

## 1. Cover

### 1.1 Annual Cover
Each beneficiary is covered for up to ₹5,00,000 per year for secondary and tertiary hospitalisation.

### 1.2 Hospitalisation-Only Scheme
The scheme covers In-patient Hospitalisation, Emergency care and the listed Day-Care packages (cataract surgery, minor surgical procedures and dialysis). Routine Out-patient (OPD) consultations and investigations that do not form part of a hospitalisation or a listed Day-Care package are NOT covered.

## 2. Pre-Existing Conditions

### 2.1 Covered From Day One
All pre-existing conditions are covered from the first day of enrolment. There is NO pre-existing disease waiting period under this scheme. A denial on the ground of a pre-existing disease waiting period is not valid under this scheme.

## 3. Pre-Authorisation

### 3.1 When Pre-Authorisation Is Required
Pre-authorisation through the scheme portal is required for every hospitalisation and for any package costing more than ₹20,000.

### 3.2 Emergencies
In an emergency, treatment must start immediately and pre-authorisation must be raised within 24 hours.

## 4. Package Limits

### 4.1 Cataract Package
Cataract surgery is reimbursed up to ₹30,000 per eye.

### 4.2 Orthopaedic Packages
Orthopaedic packages, including joint replacement, are reimbursed up to ₹2,00,000.

## 5. Claims

### 5.1 Submission
Claims must be submitted within 30 days of discharge, with the discharge summary, itemised bill and investigation reports.
