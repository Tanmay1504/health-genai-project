# Claims Procedure, Denial Standards and Grievance Redressal

FICTIONAL DOCUMENT FOR A SYNTHETIC-DATA DEMO. Applies to all plans in this demo.

## C1. Cashless and Reimbursement Claims

### C1.1 Cashless
At a network hospital, the hospital raises a pre-authorisation request. The insurer must decide on a pre-authorisation request within 1 hour of receiving complete documents, and on final discharge authorisation within 3 hours.

### C1.2 Reimbursement
The insured pays the provider and submits the claim with the mandatory documents within the time limit in the plan wording.

## C2. Documentation

### C2.1 Requests for Further Documents
If documents are incomplete, the insurer must request the specific missing documents in writing. It may not reject a claim for incomplete documentation unless it has first made such a request and the insured has failed to respond within 15 days. A claim must not be rejected for documentation that was in fact submitted with the claim.

## C3. Standards for Denial

### C3.1 Reasoned Denial
Every denial must state the specific policy clause relied upon and the facts that bring the claim within that clause. A denial that does not identify the clause, or that applies a clause to facts outside its definition, is not a valid denial.

### C3.2 Burden of Proof for Exclusions and Waiting Periods
The insurer bears the burden of proving that an exclusion or waiting period applies. For a Pre-Existing Disease denial, the insurer must show documentary evidence that the condition was diagnosed or treated before the policy start date.

### C3.3 Condonation of Delay
A claim submitted after the time limit shall be considered if the insured shows that the delay was caused by genuine hardship (for example continued hospitalisation, bereavement or the insured's incapacity). Claims are not rejected solely for delay when the delay is explained.

## C4. Appeals and Grievances

### C4.1 Right to Appeal
The insured, or the provider on the insured's behalf, may appeal a denial in writing within 30 days of the denial letter. The appeal should identify the claim, the denial reason, the policy clauses relied upon and the supporting clinical evidence.

### C4.2 Timelines
The insurer's grievance cell must acknowledge an appeal within 3 working days and decide it within 15 days. If the denial is overturned, the claim must be paid with interest for the period of delay.

### C4.3 Escalation
If the appeal is rejected or not decided in time, the insured may escalate to the applicable insurance ombudsman or the regulator's grievance portal.
