# Age-related Cataract — Indications for Surgery

Illustrative synthetic guidance written for a demo. Not medical advice.

## Indications
Cataract surgery is indicated when lens opacity reduces best-corrected visual acuity enough to impair daily activities (for example 6/12 or worse, or glare disability), or when the cataract prevents monitoring or treating other eye disease.

## Procedure
Phacoemulsification with foldable intraocular lens implantation is the standard procedure. It is performed as a day-care procedure under local anaesthesia, usually in less than an hour.

## Documentation for Authorisation
Visual acuity in both eyes, slit-lamp findings, biometry (IOL power calculation) and the surgeon's note on functional impairment.
