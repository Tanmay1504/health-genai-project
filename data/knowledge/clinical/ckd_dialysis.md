# Chronic Kidney Disease — Monitoring and Haemodialysis

Illustrative synthetic guidance written for a demo. Not medical advice.

## Staging
CKD is staged by eGFR and albuminuria. Stage 3 means an eGFR of 30–59 mL/min/1.73m². Monitoring every 3–6 months includes eGFR, potassium, haemoglobin and blood pressure.

## Dialysis
Haemodialysis is indicated for end-stage kidney disease or for refractory complications (hyperkalaemia, fluid overload, uraemic symptoms). It is a recurring day-care procedure, usually given 2–3 times a week, and is life-sustaining.

## Documentation
Serial eGFR values, the nephrologist's prescription for dialysis, and session records.
