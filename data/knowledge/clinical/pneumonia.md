# Community-acquired Pneumonia — Admission Criteria

Illustrative synthetic guidance written for a demo. Not medical advice.

## Severity Assessment
Severity is assessed with a validated score such as CURB-65 (confusion, urea > 7 mmol/L, respiratory rate ≥ 30, low blood pressure, age ≥ 65). Score 0–1: usually outpatient care. Score 2: consider a short admission. Score ≥ 3: hospital admission and possibly ICU.

## Medical Necessity of Admission
Admission is justified by hypoxaemia (SpO₂ < 92%), haemodynamic instability, inability to take oral medication, or a high severity score. A chest X-ray confirming consolidation is required.

## Red Flags in Billing
Repeated short admissions for pneumonia in young patients without hypoxaemia or radiological confirmation are not consistent with guidelines.
