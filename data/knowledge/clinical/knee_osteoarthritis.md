# Knee Osteoarthritis — Conservative Care and Arthroplasty

Illustrative synthetic guidance written for a demo. Not medical advice.

## Conservative Management
First-line care is exercise therapy, physiotherapy, weight management and analgesics. Physiotherapy is usually given as a defined course (for example 6–12 sessions) with documented functional goals. Open-ended or repeated physiotherapy without documented progress is not supported.

## Indications for Total Knee Replacement
Total knee replacement is indicated for severe pain and functional limitation despite at least 3–6 months of conservative treatment, with radiographic evidence of advanced joint-space narrowing (Kellgren–Lawrence grade 3–4).

## Documentation for Authorisation
Standing knee X-rays, a functional score (for example WOMAC), a record of failed conservative therapy, anaesthetic fitness and the surgeon's plan.
