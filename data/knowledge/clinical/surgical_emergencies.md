# Appendicitis, Fractures and Obstetric Care

Illustrative synthetic guidance written for a demo. Not medical advice.

## Acute Appendicitis
Diagnosis is clinical, supported by ultrasound or CT and a raised white-cell count. Appendectomy (laparoscopic or open) is the standard treatment and is usually urgent. Delay risks perforation and peritonitis.

## Forearm Fractures
Displaced forearm fractures need reduction (closed or open) and immobilisation, with X-rays before and after reduction. They are usually treated as an emergency.

## Caesarean Section
Caesarean delivery is indicated for obstructed labour, foetal distress, malpresentation, a previous caesarean with contraindications to labour, and other obstetric indications documented by the obstetrician.
