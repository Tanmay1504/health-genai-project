# Acute Myocardial Infarction — Emergency Revascularisation

Illustrative synthetic guidance written for a demo. Not medical advice.

## Emergency Nature
ST-elevation myocardial infarction (STEMI) is a time-critical emergency. Primary percutaneous coronary intervention (PCI) should be done as soon as possible, ideally within 90–120 minutes of first medical contact. Delaying treatment to obtain administrative approval increases mortality.

## Medical Necessity
Diagnosis is supported by ischaemic symptoms, ECG changes and raised troponin. Coronary angiography followed by PCI with stenting is the standard of care for STEMI and high-risk NSTEMI.

## Documentation
ECG tracings, troponin values, the angiography report, the stent details and the discharge summary.
