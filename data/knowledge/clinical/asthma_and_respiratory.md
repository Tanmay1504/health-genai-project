# Asthma and Acute Respiratory Infections

Illustrative synthetic guidance written for a demo. Not medical advice.

## Asthma Review
People with asthma need a structured review at least once a year, and after any exacerbation. The review covers spirometry or peak flow, inhaler technique and the action plan. Spirometry is medically necessary to confirm the diagnosis and to assess control.

## Acute Bronchitis, Pharyngitis and Sinusitis
These are usually self-limiting and managed in outpatient care. A chest X-ray is warranted when pneumonia is suspected (fever, focal chest signs, hypoxaemia). Throat culture is warranted when bacterial pharyngitis is suspected.
